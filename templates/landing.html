Everything the front desk and the consulting room need

No juggling registers, spreadsheets and billing books. Each task has a place, and every change is logged.

Patient registry

MRN, demographics, emergency contacts, allergies, chronic conditions, tags and a full timeline. Bring in old lists with CSV import.

Appointments and queue

Schedule visits, move them through a status workflow and watch the daily waiting queue. Overlapping bookings are blocked.

Clinical records

Symptoms, examination, diagnoses, treatment plans, vitals, prescriptions and follow-up dates, kept with the doctor who saw the patient.

Billing and payments

Invoices with discounts, tax and partial payments. Outstanding balances stay visible until they're settled.

Expenses and inventory

Log clinic expenses, track stock, see low-stock items and review every stock movement and adjustment.

Reports and activity history

Role-scoped reports and CSV exports, in-app notifications and an audit trail of who changed what.

Everyone sees what their job needs

Four workspace roles, each with its own limits. Billing staff never open clinical notes. Doctors never browse another doctor's patients.

Clinic Manager

Runs administration and reception from one account: patients, appointments, queue, team, invoices, expenses, inventory and reports.

Doctor

Sees their own patients, appointments, records, prescriptions, follow-ups and invoices.

Nurse

Supports patient and appointment work and views non-sensitive records. No access to billing ledgers.

Billing Staff

Handles invoices, payments and expenses. Clinical notes stay out of reach.
